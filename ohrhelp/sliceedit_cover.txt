When a slice is set to cover children, it will automatically grow or shrink so that it covers the dimensions of its children.

You can cover on the horizontal axis, the veritical axis, or both at once.

If you enable covering children and filling parent on the same axis on the same slice, then covering will be ignored, and filling will take priority.

Only immediate children are covered. Grandchildren are not covered if they go outside the bounds of the child.

Child alignment affects covering.
If a child is left-aligned, only the right side matters.
If a child is right-aligned, only the left side matters.
If a child is center-aligned, both sides matter.
